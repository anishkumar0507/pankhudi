Custom Design Journey

"Designed from scratch to match your vision"

1. Bridal Consultation

Step 1

One-on-one private styling session at our Sakchi salon to understand your ceremony moodboards, jewelry pairing, and silhouette comfort.

02

2. Design & Fabric Selection
